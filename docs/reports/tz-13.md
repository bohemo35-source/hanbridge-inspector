## What was done

### 0. Two bugs found before polishing
- **The plate background was stretched.** In `PlateScene`, `imgAspect` was hardcoded as 16/9, but the plates are 1920×1280 (3:2). The background plane was sized `w*cover × h*cover`, i.e. always in the viewport's proportions.
  - At 390×844 the photo was stretched vertically about 3× (`tz13-mobile-port-before`): containers became "towers", and the car hung in the air.
  - On desktop the error was about 7%.
  - Fix: the plane now has the exact plate proportions, plus `camera.setViewOffset`. The camera renders the plate's "full frame", and the viewport cuts a window out of it. The photo and the 3D ground always line up.
  - Dolly and pan now move the window, not the plate relative to the world, so the car no longer "slides" over the photo.
  - On narrow screens the window follows the car and the lane: `focusX` plus the car's projection. The `mx = 0.42` hack that shifted the car off the lane was removed.
- **Ramp contact.** The car now pitches to match the path tangent, and shadows and AO follow `pos.y`.

### 1. Car on plates: `PlateScene.ts`, `sceneKit.ts`, `blocks.ts`
- **HDRI instead of PMREM from the plate.** `kit.setHdri()` works as follows: `RGBELoader` (Float), values clamped at 12 (otherwise the HDRI sun overexposes the car through bloom), then PMREM and `scene.environment`.
  - New `PlateConfig` fields: `hdri`, `envRot`, `envInt` and others. Rotation and intensity were tuned with a sweep: Seoul `envRot 3.14`, Vladivostok `1.57`, `envInt` 0.3–0.4.
  - Debugging without code edits: `?envrot=&envint=&exp=&key=&rim=&fxoff=`.
- **Wet asphalt (Seoul 0.42, Port 0.36).**
  - Mirrored copy of the car: matrix `scaleY(-1)·car.matrix`, cloned materials, transparency plus alpha falloff with depth below the floor over 1.6 m (`onBeforeCompile`), slightly darker and rougher. **No blur** (deviation from the TZ: "falloff, blur and transparency") — roughness +0.25 and falloff replace it.
  - The `ShadowMaterial` floor was switched to `depthWrite:false`.
  - Headlight sprites are hidden in the reflection: they produced "specks" at the bumper.
  - Desktop only. On mobile this would double the triangles (232k → 465k).
- **Headlights.** Instead of circles: light pools on the road (additive beam-shaped decals), volumetric cones (shader: fresnel plus falloff toward the tip), and red brake-light glow on the asphalt.
  - When the car faces away from the camera (rear toward it), headlight sprites are hidden and the cones and pools are weakened.
  - Deviation from the TZ: decals were made instead of a `SpotLight`. A `SpotLight` would light nothing here: the ground is a `ShadowMaterial` catcher, and the photo is unlit `MeshBasic`.
- **Ground contact.** A dense AO patch under the body (rounded rectangle 4.6×2.0 m — after review: the first version was 4.0×1.33 m, the car's own size, and it was hidden under the car), 4 dark spots under the wheels, a long soft shadow along the key light direction (on mobile opacity 0.45, where there is no shadow map). Shadow map raised to 2048.
- **The backlight from the TZ was built and then disabled.**
  - The directional rim light from behind reflected in the glossy glass and, through bloom, produced a white spot. Verified by isolation: `?rim=0` and `?key=0`, frames `t-rim0*` in the scratchpad.
  - The HDRI provides the silhouette separation. The rim light is kept only as a debug option `?rim=`.
- **Rain in Seoul:** 380 thin additive streaks (160 on mobile) in camera space.
- **Color grading:** new `FilmGrade.tsx`. It consists of a vignette, a soft-light tone of the plate (`grade`), and animated CSS grain (SVG feTurbulence; no animation under `prefers-reduced-motion`). It is overlaid on the 3D blocks and bridges, so the car and the photo read as one image.
- The point light `carLight` inside the body was removed: it lit the roof from the inside.

### 2. Garage: `GarageScene.tsx`
- Reflections and ambient come from `autoshop_01_2k.hdr` (`1k` on mobile), values clamped at 16. The visible background is unchanged (Muse Image panorama).
- Lighting:
  - top softbox `RectAreaLight` 6.5×3.2;
  - two cool backlight `SpotLight`s low at the back, one on each side;
  - exposure left at 1.3.
- Part details: metallic paint (`metalness 0.5`, `clearcoat 1`, `clearcoatRoughness 0.08`). Inner faces are dark and matte via `DoubleSide` and `onBeforeCompile` (`!gl_FrontFacing`: color 0.025, roughness 1, metal 0, clearcoat 0) — without duplicating meshes. Cabin parts get a wrapper on every `clone()`, because `clone()` drops `onBeforeCompile`.
- Floor: `Reflector` with a custom shader (9-tap blur, strength 0.45), epoxy texture with opacity 0.82. The reflection is readable but not mirror-like.
- Beat logic, data and labels were not changed.

### 3. Road from above: `RoadScene.tsx`
- 4 Muse Image aerial photos taken straight down: coast, taiga, steppe, suburb. Script `scripts/journey-v2/generate-aerials.mjs`; prompts added to `prompts.json`.
- The road centerline was measured on each photo: automatic search for the grey strip across 5 rows, then least squares. Check: the line over the photo (`/tmp/claude-501/lines.png`). The road width in px sets the car's scale.
- Scene:
  - photos are attached to the camera, and the window slides along the road;
  - the car, seen from above, sits at the center of the window and is rotated to follow the road;
  - sun shadow and blob shadow;
  - a golden route trail behind the car;
  - transition between zones — a crossfade over 20% of the zone, smoothly blending the road angle and camera height.
- Finale — a dark map: grid and speckles, glowing route tubes (Vladivostok → Novosibirsk → Moscow, Almaty → Tashkent branch), glowing city dots, labels with glow. The `CITIES` entries were not changed.
- Old 3D trees and tiles were removed.

### 4. Transitions: `useStageFade.ts`, `SceneBlock.tsx`, `Bridge.tsx`, `GarageScene.tsx`, `JourneyV2.tsx`
- Every pinned block after the first starts `LEAD_VH = 118vh` earlier (`margin-top: -118vh`, height `+118vh`), so the page height does not change.
  - Its scene gets pinned 18vh (≈0.5 s) before the previous one releases, and fades in on top of it.
  - Both scenes dim during this, to a maximum of 0.45; at the handover before the reviews — 0.6.
  - Previously the next block slid in from below over 100vh with a hard edge.
- While a block is transparent, `pointer-events: none` is set on both the scene and the section itself. Originally it was only on the scene, and the next block's section (margin −118vh) intercepted clicks on "Open report" in the garage — a blocker from review, fixed in `8ee86e1`. See the hit-test below.
- The HUD and caption now take the active block from the DOM: a block counts as current from the midpoint of its fade-in. Previously they came from a vh-based formula. Cause: the page is 5400vh against a block sum of 5500vh — an old mismatch that made the HUD run ~100vh ahead of the picture.
- `blockAt` remains as a fallback; tests were not changed.
- `SceneBlock`: on a scroll jump, the 2-canvas limit triggered before the old slot was released, and the poster stayed (this is how the finale map and the handover went missing). Now the block retries mounting on scroll and on the "slot freed" event (`onCanvasFreed` in `sceneKit`). Only the block holding a slot (`holdsSlot`) gives it back; a stale mount (`gen`, `!near` after `await`) also gives its slot back.
- `sceneKit.dispose` now frees `map` on mesh and sprite materials (canvas textures of decals, labels, the map); previously they leaked.
- The garage verdict card on desktop was raised above the HUD buttons (`sm:bottom-24`). The overlap also existed on `main`; found by the reviewer.

### Not done / deviations
- **Blur of the reflection on wet asphalt is not done.** TZ item 1 asks for "falloff, blur and transparency". Transparency 0.36–0.42, depth falloff over 1.6 m, and roughness +0.25 were done; a real blur (render to texture + blur) is not.
- **Car motion blur is not done.** A trail or a second copy would add another +232k triangles, and a full-frame blur (AfterimagePass) would also smear the plate. Advice from the reviewer-advisor: "blur only the car, not the plate." I chose not to do it. Motion is conveyed by vibration, rain, the sliding aerial photos and crossfades.
- **Headlight spotlight** replaced with decals (reason above). Backlight in the plates is disabled (reason above).
- **Wet reflection on desktop only.** On mobile, the headlight pools, brake lights and AO remain.
- **No before/after pairs for transitions:** "before" is the hard slide-in; a mid-crossfade frame exists only for "after".
- **Mobile road:** no "before" frame; it was not in the TZ-12 set for this viewport.

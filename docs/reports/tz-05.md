Bottlenecks from the trace: `read_gauge_video` 72.7 s (batches of Spark frames + SAM re-read), `transcribe_note` 26–40 s (ASR on 15-second chunks), `make_listing_photo` up to 31 s (Muse Image edits; on MINI and X3 rejected by the diff guard >10 — an honest fallback, background untouched).

- If a finding is confirmed only by the gauge screen color, it goes to Unconfirmed (`conflict_check`).
- Listing photos:

  - the photo is searched among 24 evenly chosen ones, in batches of 6;
  - plates of other cars in the background are blurred;
  - if no such photo exists, the listing is not published (the report uses the hero from Muse Image).

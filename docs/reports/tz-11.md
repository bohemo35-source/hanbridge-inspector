## What was done

- **Block chain** (`src/components/journey-v2/`): `blocks.ts` (order, vh heights, pure `blockAt`/`stageForBlock`/`startProgressOf`), `copy.ts` (RU/EN), `useCar.ts` (one cached GLTF `car-v2.glb` per page, clone + 4.4 m normalization, headlight sprites, body vibration instead of wheel rotation), `sceneKit.ts` (ACES, PMREM environment, bloom on desktop only, eco pixelRatio, `dispose` + `forceContextLoss`, triangle counter, semaphore of max 2 canvases), `SceneBlock.tsx` (mounts within 1 screen, unmounts beyond 2 screens).
- **3D blocks**: Seoul (night cable-stayed bridge, Points lights on the shores, car on the bridge, camera rises), Port Busan (InstancedMesh containers, 2 cranes, wet asphalt texture, ro-ro ship, ramp), Vladivostok (rounded hill spheres + panorama with a cable-stayed bridge, ship at the quay, car drives off), Long Road (camera from above, 3 landscape textures, instanced trees, route line drawn on the ground; at the end a rise to a map with labels Vladivostok/Novosibirsk/Almaty/Tashkent/"Moscow ~9 000 km · 12–14 days"), Handover (golden hour, client's garage, headlights go out, route line closes the loop).
- **Garage inside**: `<GarageScene report={MINI} lang />` with no component changes; the report is the latest MINI report from the server (as in `/cars`), with a "Demo report" link → `/r/:id`.
- **Bridges** (`Bridge.tsx`): sticky full-screen Muse Image picture, slow push-in + parallax, glass cards from `STAGE_CONTENT` (numbers unchanged), at customs — a "Cleared" stamp. The HUD caption is hidden on the bridges and in the garage (they have their own cards/captions).
- **HUD**: stages Purchase → Inspection → Documents → Port → Sea → Arrival → Customs → Delivery (on the right; at the top on mobile), click = smooth scroll, RU/EN (`?lang=ru`, default EN), "Find a car" always visible; under the caption — "After payment, to Moscow — about 4–5 weeks".
- **Reviews**: the reviewer's manifest was found (`public/reviews/manifest.json`, 3 real reviews) — a block of 3 cards, muted/looping videos, autoplay on visibility (`Reviews.tsx`); if the manifest is empty or missing, the block is hidden (test + filter in `page.tsx`). I did not touch the files in `public/reviews/*`.
- **Fallback**: `prefers-reduced-motion` / no WebGL2 / `?no3d=1` — a vertical strip with one Muse Image frame per block, with the same captions; the garage has its own fallback.
- **Lab**: `/lab/journey-v2`.

I did not touch other processes; build+capture were done in the git worktree `/tmp/hb-tz11` of the same commit with its own `.next` (worktree removed after capture). After that I did not re-run `npm run build` in the main copy — build evidence is from the worktree.
- **Duration ~2:05 instead of 2:45**: the sum of block heights per the TZ = 4250vh (actually 4150vh because the reviews section uses `min-height`); at the norm of 3 s/100vh the target length is short. It is fixed by increasing block heights (500→650vh, etc.) — the height figures were taken from the TZ table unchanged.
- **Port**: the car drives toward the ship, but the ramp geometry and the entry point into the hull only approximately match: mid-block the car is at the stern, and toward the end it goes inside and is hidden by the hull; there is no exact wheel placement on the ramp.
- **Vladivostok**: the car on the quay looks small mid-block; the ship is a simplified hull without details.
- **Garage in capture** crashed the Chrome tab when scrolling through several WebGL blocks in a row in one browser — capture takes blocks one process at a time (`ONLY=`/`SKIP_EXTRA=1`). In normal user scrolling, blocks are unmounted on removal (verified: `canvases` ≤ 2).
- **Mobile Seoul**: the caption at the bottom partly overlaps the RU→EN button; the car is larger than the frame on the left.
- FPS was measured only on desktop Chrome (GPU metal); mobile FPS — the same headless run with a 390×844 viewport (60), not a physical phone.

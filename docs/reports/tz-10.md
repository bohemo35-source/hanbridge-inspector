## What is in the scene (`/lab/garage`)
- Per-part model: 32 parts, 180 132 triangles (`data-triangles`). Own materials:
  - body — satin varnish, which hides the noise of the generated surface;
  - cabin — black gloss;
  - wheels, underbody — dark matte;
  - headlights — emissive.
- Environment in code:
  - floor — Reflector under a matte layer;
  - LED strips and RectAreaLight;
  - warm rim light;
  - haze cones;
  - wall with cabinets in half-darkness;
  - scissor lift.
- Bloom is enabled on desktop. Eco mode (fps < 40 over 2 s) turns off reflection, bloom and haze. Mobile — no Reflector.
- 6 beats driven by scroll:
  1. drive-in;
  2. lift;
  3. disassembly by directions from the parts map;
  4. top view: labels with real µm values from the MINI report, hood 332 µm "filler/repair · ✓ confirmed";
  5. OBD (MINI has no data, so this beat shows reassembly with the caption "Assembling back…"; no numbers are invented);
  6. NEGOTIATE verdict "3 findings · 1 confirmed" and a link to `/r/:id`.
- No WebGL or `prefers-reduced-motion` → a strip of 6 cards with the same data.

## Reviewer fixes after Muse Wonder
| Problem in Muse Wonder's screenshots | Fix |
|---|---|
| Two-post lift columns blocked the car in almost every frame | Scissor lift without columns |
| In beat 3 parts overlapped and labels collided | Wider spread; front and rear doors separated along X; camera from above (schematic view) |
| Body looked "chrome", generation noise was obvious | Dark graphite, satin varnish |
| Hood labelled "not confirmed" although the hood filler is confirmed | Label: measurement class + "✓ confirmed" if there is a confirmed finding; "not confirmed" only if none are confirmed |
| Beat 4 for a car without OBD hung disassembled with an empty caption | Without OBD, beat 4 = reassembly with caption |
| White glare on the floor in beats 4–5 and a white lift plate | Floor without env reflection and rough (the mirror comes from Reflector); lift matte |
| LED strip covered the car in the top view | Strips and cones hidden when the camera is above the ceiling |
| Script captured via software GL (0.5 fps), some frames black | On Mac — real GPU (`--use-angle=metal`): 60 fps; software GL only with `CHROMIUM_PATH` |

I tried smoothing normals (`mergeVertices` + `computeVertexNormals`): on the quantized meshopt geometry the scene rendered black. Reverted.

## Not done / limitations
- Generation left a light "waviness" on the surface. Satin varnish hides it, but close-ups of highlights on the side panels reveal it. Looks best in 3/4 and top views.
- The front left fender is not a separate part (it is in the body `tripo_part_0`). Its measurement is tied to the body.
- The textured model `hero-car.glb` is not used yet: its axis was rotated after `align_image`, and the time went to the main scene.
- `/lab/garage` returns 404 in production (intended by Muse Wonder). Integrating the scene into the homepage journey is the next TZ.
- The console shows 404s for some dev-server resources (not the models: `data-parts=32` loads). Not investigated.

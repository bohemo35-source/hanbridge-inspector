## Goal
A cinematic scroll scene that shows the client the route of their car after the inspection: the same car from the 3D body template (TZ-06) drives from Korea across the bridge and through the port, sails by sea, arrives in Vladivostok, and leaves by road to RF/CIS. The stages are tied to data: the client's current stage is highlighted from `get_shipping_status`. The stage backgrounds are generated by **Muse Image** (Best Creative nomination); the geometry is built in code.

## Hard rules (hackathon)
- Everything is written from scratch. No code, models, textures or assets from Hanbridge Auto (Bolt), Landing_Exportal_muse, Hanbridge_3D_lab, Kimi, unitedcarriers — only the idea.
- The car is only `buildCar(bodyType)` from `src/components/damage-map-3d/buildCar.ts` (import it, do not copy it). No logos or recognizable brands.
- Bridges, port, ship, city — generic, low-poly, built in code (no one-to-one copies of real architectural objects).
- Rasters come only from Muse Image of this project; the prompts are saved in the repo (provenance).

## What to do
1. `src/components/journey-3d/`:
   - `route.ts` — the route as a `CatmullRomCurve3` through 7 anchor zones; `stageAt(progress: 0..1): StageId`, `progressOf(stage)`; pure functions, no three.js rendering.
   - `scenery.ts` — generators: road with markings (instanced), cable-stayed bridge (pylons + stay cables as lines), cranes/containers (InstancedMesh), ro-ro ship (simple volumes), water (ShaderMaterial: waves in the vertex shader, Fresnel), hills (noise heightfield), city lights (Points). Budget for the whole scene `< 250 000` triangles.
   - `JourneyScene.tsx` — `"use client"`, Three.js. Section scroll (sticky, height 700vh) → `progress` → the camera moves along the curve (smoothed), the car moves along its own curve (at stage `sea` it stands on the ship's deck). Light and fog change per stage (night → morning in Vladivostok). The stage caption (RU/EN per `lang`) fades in/out with progress. The stage background — a plane/dome with a texture from `public/journey/<stage>.jpg`, cross-fading between stages.
   - props: `{ bodyType, doors?, lang, currentStage?: StageId, compact?: boolean }`. `currentStage` → a "Your car is here" marker (RU copy lives in src/components/journey-3d/stageContent.ts) and a pulse on that stage; stages after the current one are slightly dimmed.
   - Fallback without WebGL and with `prefers-reduced-motion`: a vertical strip of 7 cards (stage background + caption + current-stage mark).
   - Eco: average FPS over 2 s < 40 → turn off the shader waves (static water), simpler fog, pixelRatio 1. Mobile: pixelRatio ≤ 1.5, section height 500vh.
2. Muse Image backdrops: `scripts/journey/generate-backdrops.mjs` — 7 requests `POST /images/generations` (`muse-image-1.0`, ~23 s each, sequential), saves `public/journey/<stage>.jpg` (≤ 1600 px, JPEG q80) and `public/journey/prompts.json` (prompt, model, date). One unified style: cinematic, dusk/dawn, soft haze, no people, no text, no logos and no recognizable brands. Run once; the images are committed.
3. Data link: `src/lib/agent/tools/shipping.ts` — `getShippingStatus(reportId)` returns `{ stage: StageId, etaDays?: number, source: "demo_fixture" }` from `src/lib/agent/fixtures/shipping.json` (the 5 demo cars have different stages). The agent uses the same tool in TZ-05.
4. Where to show it:
   - home page `/`: a hero section with `JourneyScene` (hatchback car, no `currentStage`), the product title on top and a button "Inspection catalog" (RU copy lives in src/components/journey-3d/stageContent.ts) → `/cars`;
   - `/lab/journey?stage=<id>&body=<type>&lang=ru|en` — a demo page for recording the video;
   - for TZ-05: export `<JourneyScene compact currentStage=… />` for the "Delivery" tab in `/r/:id` (TZ-05 will connect it).
5. Tests (vitest, no network and no WebGL): `stageAt`/`progressOf` (boundaries and monotonicity), all 7 stages reachable, `getShippingStatus` returns a valid stage for each demo car, triangle budget of the generators (`< 250 000`).
6. Screenshots: extend `scripts/3d/capture.mjs` (or a new `scripts/journey/capture.mjs`) — frames of each stage via `/lab/journey?stage=…`, a mobile frame, and the fallback `?no3d=1`.

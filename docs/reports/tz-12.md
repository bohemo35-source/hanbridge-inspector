# TZ-12 Report — Journey v3: photo plates + 3D car

Branch `tz-11` (as specified in the TZ), commits `75c9555` (plates + SAM), `0d62ffd` (plate mode), `6e14b7a` (calibration) + a final commit with the report. Not pushed.

## What was done

- **Plates** (`scripts/journey-v2/generate-plates.mjs`, appends to `public/journey-v2/prompts.json`): 4 frames with no cars in the foreground, horizon ~42%, eye level 1.5 m: `plate-seoul-bridge.jpg`, `plate-port-ramp.jpg` (ro-ro stern with a ramp and a glowing hold on the right), `plate-vladivostok-quay.jpg` (hills, cable-stayed bridge in haze, ship with a ramp), `plate-handover.jpg` (house with garage, golden hour).
- **Compositing** (`src/components/journey-v2/scenes/PlateScene.ts`, `plate` mode in `SceneBlock`): background — a plane in camera space (cover, parallax ±2% and a +4% push-in per block); `environment` — the same plate via PMREM (applied after the texture loads — a synchronous call crashed the scene); directional light + hemisphere tint matched to the plate (night cold / dawn and golden hour warm); invisible floor `ShadowMaterial` (opacity from config 0.38–0.48) + radial contact shadow under the car; the car `car-v2.glb` moves along the CatmullRom path from `blocks.ts` (PLATES), with body vibration; at night, headlights (radial glow sprite in `useCar.ts` — previously a white square appeared in front) + PointLight; at handover the headlights go out at p>0.82, and the rear sprites blink at 0.88<p<0.94. Debug mode `?debug=plate`: GridHelper + yellow path line + red dots (`tz12-debug-plate-*`).
- **Port / hold**: SAM 3.1 with the concept `ship ramp doorway` produced a ramp box (see below); a usable opening mask for occlusion did not come out, so the fallback allowed by the TZ was applied: at the end of the path (p>0.78) the car shrinks to 0.05 and darkens/disappears at the opening. End frame — `tz12-05b-port-end.png`: the car is small at the top of the ramp by the opening.
- **Code-scene fallback**: the old `SeoulScene/PortScene/VladivostokScene/FinaleScene` were not deleted; `SceneBlock` switches to them when plate loading fails; the block poster is the plate itself.
- **Road from above** stays 3D: tiles enlarged to 260 m, 4 zones (coast → forest → steppe → muted suburban steppe, different repeat/tint), `receiveShadow` + contact shadow behind the car; finale map unchanged. Garage and bridges unchanged.
- **TZ-11 review**: the bottom caption is not shown on the bridges (verified on `tz12-06-sea.png`: only cards at right/bottom, HUD "Sea"); HUD: reviews and finale — "Delivery" (code + block-order test). Heights increased: sum of `BLOCKS` = **5500vh** (Sea bridge 450→750, "Sourcing"/documents 300→400, Seoul 500→650, Port 450→600, road 450→700). Actual page height in the screenshots: 48 600 px at a 900 px viewport = **5400vh** (finale/reviews on min-height); at the norm 100vh≈3 s this is ≈ **2:42–2:45**.

## Not achieved / limitations

- **Hold occlusion with the SAM mask was not applied**: SAM returned the ramp mask, not the edge of the opening; the entry is done by shrinking to 0.05 + disappearing at the opening (the fallback allowed by the TZ). On `tz12-05b-port-end.png` the car stands at the left edge of the opening, not centered in the glowing hold: the perspective of the flat plate does not allow moving the 3D car exactly to the center of the opening without distorting the path in the middle of the block.
- **Shadow**: ShadowMaterial works only on desktop (shadowMap is disabled on mobile in sceneKit); on mobile only the contact blob remains. On the wet dark asphalt of Seoul/Port the blob is barely visible — grounding is read from the wheels and the plate's reflections.
- **Plate perspective — an approximation**: ground y=0 and the path were tuned on the debug grid, but the photo was not shot from our camera; in Vladivostok the car mid-block is larger than the "photo scale" of the quay (it is closer to our camera than the ramp in the photo).
- **Build in the main copy**: a live server on :3115 corrupted `.next` during a rebuild (500s) — fixed by stopping the server before `npm run build`; the final build ran cleanly, and the capture server was started after it.
- FPS — desktop Chrome only (GPU metal) and the mobile viewport in the same headless run; no physical phone was used.
- `CONTEXT.md`/`PROJECT-STATUS.md` were not updated: TZ-12 allows touching only the listed paths; the status for TZ-11/12 is in this report and the TZ-11 report.
